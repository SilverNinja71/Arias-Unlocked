Composer Spotlight

Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

1756 – 1791 · Salzburg, Austria

Mozart transformed opera from rigid aristocratic entertainment into psychological drama bursting with wit, youth, and human vulnerability.

Key Masterpieces for Beginners:

  • Le nozze di Figaro ("Non so più", "Voi che sapete")
  • Don Giovanni ("Là ci darem la mano")
  • Die Zauberflöte (The Magic Flute)
