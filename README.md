# Arias Unlocked

Promoting the comprehension and appreciation of classical music and opera to youth.

## Pages & Structure

- [**Home (`index.html`)**](file:///Users/lin/Google%20Drive/Antonio/Home/Other/Google/Learning/Python/Arias-Unlocked/index.html): Hero showcase, curated journey bento grid, composer spotlights, and quick access.
- [**Songs & Repertoire (`songs.html`)**](file:///Users/lin/Google%20Drive/Antonio/Home/Other/Google/Learning/Python/Arias-Unlocked/songs.html): Comprehensive classical aria library with real-time search, multi-tag filtering, tag expansion, audio previews, and side-by-side original lyrics with English translations.
- [**The Green Room Blog (`blog.html`)**](file:///Users/lin/Google%20Drive/Antonio/Home/Other/Google/Learning/Python/Arias-Unlocked/blog.html): Youth-oriented essays on classical interpretation, vocal technique, and repertoire with an interactive reading modal and newsletter signup.
- [**About Me (`about.html`)**](file:///Users/lin/Google%20Drive/Antonio/Home/Other/Google/Learning/Python/Arias-Unlocked/about.html): Mission statement, three core pillars, and a song suggestion form.

## Interactive Features

- **Real-Time Search & Multi-Tag Filter**: Instant search by title, composer, language, emotion, or voice type.
- **Listen & Read Modal**: Synthesizes classical motifs, plays previews, and presents side-by-side lyrics and youth listening guides.
- **Interactive Article Reader**: Full reading modal for Green Room essays.
- **Join Community & Newsletter**: Interactive modals with local storage persistence.
