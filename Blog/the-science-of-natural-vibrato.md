# The Science of Natural Vibrato
**Category:** Technique  
**Date:** Sep 01, 2024  
**Read Time:** 6 min read  
**Author:** Antonio  

---

Vibrato is not an intentional shaking of the jaw or diaphragm; it is the physiological byproduct of a balanced, resonant phonation where antagonistic muscle groups exist in dynamic equilibrium. Understanding the acoustic mechanics helps young students sing freely without artificial tension.
