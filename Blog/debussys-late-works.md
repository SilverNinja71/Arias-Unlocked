# Debussy's Late Works
**Category:** Repertoire  
**Date:** Sep 20, 2024  
**Read Time:** 6 min read  
**Author:** Antonio  

---

Claude Debussy's final three sonatas (for cello, flute-viola-harp, and violin) are often categorized under hazy impressionist umbrellas. In truth, they represent a fiercely lucid return to French classical order and neoclassical symmetry. Written against the harrowing backdrop of World War I, these pieces strip away all orchestral excess, leaving crystalline motivic counterpoint and unforgettable harmonic yearning.
