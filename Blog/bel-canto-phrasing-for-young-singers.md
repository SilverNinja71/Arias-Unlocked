# Bel Canto Phrasing for Young Singers
**Category:** Vocalization  
**Date:** Sep 15, 2024  
**Read Time:** 5 min read  
**Author:** Antonio  

---

Bel canto literally translates to 'beautiful singing', but to young vocalists it often feels elusive. The secret lies in vowel alignment. By cultivating an unwavering continuity of sound—what the old Italian masters called 'legato'—the vocal chords vibrate with optimal efficiency without squeezing. Every phrase becomes an arching ribbon of light.
