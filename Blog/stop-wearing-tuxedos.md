# Stop Wearing Tuxedos
**Category:** Tips  
**Date:** Sep 28, 2024  
**Read Time:** 4 min read  
**Author:** Antonio  

---

Why are young concertgoers intimidated by classical halls? Often, it starts before a single note is played. The Victorian penguin suit creates an unspoken psychological distance between audience and performer. Contemporary concert halls in Berlin, London, and Tokyo are experimenting with modern monochrome tailoring and relaxed dress codes. When the visual aesthetic feels alive and relevant, young audiences can connect directly with the raw emotional truth of the music.
