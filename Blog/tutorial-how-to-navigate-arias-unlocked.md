Welcome to **Arias Unlocked**! Whether you are an aspiring bel canto singer, an opera connoisseur, or exploring classical repertoire for the first time, this platform was built for you.

### 1. Exploring Songs & Repertoire
Head over to the **Songs** tab to explore a curated archive of world-renowned classical arias, art songs, and vocal mechanics. Filter by voice type (Soprano, Tenor, Baritone, Mezzo, Bass), language, or composer (Mozart, Puccini, Verdi, Handel, and more).

### 2. Reading Essays, Tracking Levels & Earning XP
Every time you read an essay in **The Green Room**, you advance your personal vocal knowledge:
- **Reading an Article:** Earns **+15 XP** and advances your reading level from *Opera Novice* to *Bel Canto Connoisseur* and *Opera Maestro*.
- **Tracking Your Stats:** Click the **Profile Icon** in the top right at any time to see how many articles you have read, your current level, and your progress toward the next rank.

### 3. Suggesting Articles & The 3-Qualification AI Review
Anyone in the community can write and suggest an essay! Click **Suggest a Post (AI Review)** to write in our in-browser editor. Before publishing, our AI evaluates your draft in real-time on 3 strict qualifications:
1. **Factuality:** Historical composer citations (such as Mozart, Puccini, Verdi, Debussy) and accurate vocal mechanics (legato, appoggio, resonance, timbre).
2. **Relevance:** Keeping the focus strictly centered on classical singing, opera, and musicianship.
3. **Vocabulary (Zero Swear Words):** Zero profanity allowed—enforcing polite, courteous, artistic vocabulary.

**XP Rewards:** You earn **+50 XP** just for writing a post. If your post passes AI review and gets accepted, you get **double the XP (+100 XP bonus)!**

### 7. Joining the Conversation
