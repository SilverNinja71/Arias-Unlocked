# Acting with Your Voice: Beyond the Words
**Category:** Interpretation  
**Date:** Sep 08, 2024  
**Read Time:** 4 min read  
**Author:** Antonio  

---

On the operatic stage, subtle facial expressions cannot always be seen from the amphitheater. The voice must do the heavy lifting. In Mozart's comic arias, notice how the slightest darkening of the timbre or crisp staccato immediately signals mischief to the listener.
